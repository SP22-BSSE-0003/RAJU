🎯 SCORING SYSTEM
=================

BASE: 100 points
TIME BONUS:
  <10s: +60 pts
  10-20s: +50 pts
  20-30s: +30 pts
  30-60s: +10 pts

MATCH TYPES:
  Exact: 100 + bonus
  Fuzzy (typo): 75 pts
  Synonym: 50 pts
  Wrong: 0 pts
